The Food City 500 at Bristol Motor Speedway

500 laps of pure, high-banked adrenaline in the hills of East Tennessee

Few spectacles in American motorsports rival the intensity of the annual Food City 500 at Bristol Motor Speedway. Since its debut in 1961, Bristol's spring race weekend has forged legends, ignited iconic rivalries, and delivered some of the most heart-stopping finishes in NASCAR history. With the spring race restored to Bristol's celebrated high-banked concrete surface, fans are treated to 500 laps of blistering side-by-side racing on 28-degree banking in the turns and 6 to 10-degree banking on the straights.

The Food City 500 race weekend is a multi-day motorsport festival. The schedule typically showcases a high-octane tripleheader featuring the NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series, the NASCAR Xfinity Series, and the main event: Sunday's grueling NASCAR Cup Series Food City 500. Across the entire weekend, engines roar, tires screech, and paint is exchanged at over 120 miles per hour inside "The Last Great Colosseum" — an arena that holds more than 140,000 cheering fans.

When you camp at Bristol Hilltop Camping, you are placed right in the heart of this electric atmosphere. You can wake up to the smell of morning coffee and race fuel, listen to the warm-ups echo through the Holston Valley, and walk to your seat in 15 minutes flat.

Spring Camping Guide: Weather Tips for the Food City 500

How to stay warm, dry, and comfortable during April in East Tennessee

Springtime in the Appalachian foothills of East Tennessee is breathtaking, with budding dogwood trees, blooming mountain wildflowers, and crisp mountain air. If you are planning for camping near Bristol Motor Speedway April race weekend, preparing for the seasonal climate will ensure your Food City 500 camping experience is flawless and comfortable from setup to tear-down.

🧥 1. Master the Art of Layering

April temperatures in Bristol regularly swing 30 to 40 degrees in a single day. Daytime highs often hit a comfortable 65°F to 75°F under bright sunshine, while night temperatures can drop into the upper 30s or 40s°F. Pack a combination of moisture-wicking base layers, fleece pullovers, and a wind-resistant jacket. You will appreciate the extra layers during morning track walks and evening campfires!

🚐 2. Early-Season RV Readiness

For many campers, the Food City 500 marks their first major RV trip of the spring season. Before pulling out of your driveway, de-winterize your plumbing, sanitize your freshwater tank, test your water pump, and inspect your propane system. Fill both LP tanks so your furnace can keep your cabin cozy on chilly spring nights. It is also wise to pack a heated or well-insulated freshwater drinking hose in case nighttime temperatures flirt with the freezing mark.

🌧️ 3. Rain Gear & Track Rules (No Umbrellas in Grandstands)

Spring mountain showers can pass through quickly. Bring sturdy ground tarps, waterproof boots or hiking shoes, and heavy-duty rain ponchos. Remember that Bristol Motor Speedway prohibits umbrellas inside the grandstands because they obstruct the sightlines of fellow fans. A high-quality hooded rain poncho will keep you completely dry while watching high-speed qualifying or race action.

🔥 4. Cozy Campfires & Evening Tailgating

Nothing beats sitting around a roaring campfire on a cool April evening under the starry Tennessee sky. Above-ground fire pits are welcome at Bristol Hilltop Camping. Pick up dry, seasoned local firewood to avoid transporting invasive pests, bring roasting sticks for s'mores, and enjoy swapping racing stories with fellow NASCAR diehards.
