🏁 What to Bring & Race Weekend Insider Tips

Get the most out of your Bass Pro Shops Night Race camping trip with this veteran checklist

Essential Gear for Bristol Night Race Campers

Bristol Motor Speedway is famously dubbed "The World's Fastest Half-Mile" and "The Last Great Colosseum." With 36 degrees of banking and over 120,000 cheering fans packed into a concrete stadium bowl, it is one of the loudest, most thrilling environments in sports. A little preparation makes your weekend unforgettable:

  • Hearing Protection & Racing Scanners: Decibel levels at Bristol can reach upwards of 130 dB. Bring foam earplugs or over-ear earmuffs for everyone in your party, especially children. Rent or bring a race scanner (Racing Electronics) so you can listen to spotter chatter, crew chiefs, and the MRN race broadcast live.
  • Comfortable Walking Shoes: You are just 0.9 miles from the track gates! The walk down Hilltop Street and across Volunteer Parkway is brisk and convenient, but you will want supportive walking shoes or broken-in sneakers.
  • Layered Clothing for Mountain Temperatures: Mid-September in Bristol, Tennessee brings pleasant daytime highs in the mid-70s to low-80s, but once the sun goes down over Sullivan County, temperatures can drop rapidly into the 50s. Bring hoodies, light jackets, and long pants for the 500-lap night race finish.
  • Speedway-Approved Soft-Sided Coolers: Bristol Motor Speedway allows fans to bring one soft-sided cooler (maximum dimensions: 14" × 14" × 14") plus one lightweight bag or backpack into the grandstands. No glass containers are permitted inside the track.
  • Camp Shade & Seating: Pop-up 10x10 canopies, folding camp chairs, patio mats, and battery-powered camp lanterns will make your campsite the ultimate pre-race tailgating lounge between track sessions.
  • RV Essentials: If booking a full hookup RV site, bring a standard 25–50 ft RV drinking water hose, water pressure regulator, 30A or 50A electrical surge protector/cord, and heavy-duty sewer hose with proper elbow fittings.

💡 Pro Tips from Your Camp Hosts

1. Arrive Early on Thursday or Friday

Check in early on Thursday or Friday morning. You will avoid race-day traffic on Hwy 394, set up your campsite at leisure, and catch the UNOH 200 Truck race and Food City 300 Xfinity battle before Saturday's main event.

2. Pack Clear Bags for Quick Entry

BMS security checkpoints move fast, but clear tote bags or clutches ensure you breeze through security without delay.

3. Stay Hydrated

Between cheering, walking, and tailgating, keep plenty of bottled water in your cooler. September sun can be deceivingly warm before the evening cool rolls in.

4. Reserve Your Spot Now

Full hookup spots for the night race are in high demand nationwide. Don't wait until race week when sites are completely gone.
