Welcome to Bristol Hilltop Camping

Nestled in the beautiful rolling hills of Bristol, Tennessee, Bristol Hilltop Camping is a family-owned and operated campground offering monthly and yearly camper lot rentals. Whether you're looking for a long-term home base, a seasonal retreat, or a place to park during race weekends at Bristol Motor Speedway, our hilltop location has you covered.

Located at 133 Hilltop Street, just 0.9 miles from Thunder Valley, we offer full hookup RV sites and tent camping spaces with the flexibility of monthly or annual leases. Many of our long-term residents love the convenience of living minutes from the speedway, local shops, and the scenic Appalachian countryside.
