Welcome to Bristol Hilltop Camping

Nestled in the beautiful rolling hills of Bristol, Tennessee, Bristol Hilltop Camping is a family-owned and operated campground that has been welcoming campers from across the country. Whether you're here for the roar of engines at Bristol Motor Speedway or the peace of the Appalachian mountains, our hilltop location offers the best of both worlds.

Located at 133 Hilltop Street, we're just a short distance from one of NASCAR's most iconic tracks — Thunder Valley. Our campground is the ideal home base for race weekends, offering full hookup RV sites and tent camping spaces with the convenience of being mere minutes from the speedway gates.

🏆 Race Weekend Favorite: Our sites are highly sought after during the Food City 500 and the Bass Pro Shops Night Race. Limited full hookup sites available — reserve yours early!
